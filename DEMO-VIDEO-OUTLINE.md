# aural. — demo video outline

This is a plan for recording the TAMU Datathon 2026 demo video. **No video has been recorded or included in this repository yet.** Once one exists, add its link to the README.

Target length: about 2 minutes. Record the site running at `http://localhost:8000/app/` (or the published URL) in a desktop browser window at 1440 px or wider, with system audio captured.

## Preparation

- Record a short, dry voice clip beforehand (for example, one spoken sentence with a brief pause in the middle) and keep it ready to upload. Using the same clip every take keeps the comparison fair.
- Use headphones while recording so the room previews do not feed back into the microphone.
- Close other tabs and notifications.

## Accuracy reminders for the narration

- Call the values **estimates from handclaps**, not certified measurements.
- Collection facts you can state: November 7, 2026; Apple iPhone built-in microphone; Shure MOTIV; 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono WAV; phone on a stand about 5 ft from the source; same settings throughout. Do not state the iPhone model, recording times, exact positions or room dimensions; they were not recorded.
- Blocker Staircase (clap) and Blocker Stairwell (flute) are the same stairwell. Describe them as two samples of one place, not two spaces.
- Say "approximation" when describing how the Studio output relates to the real room.
